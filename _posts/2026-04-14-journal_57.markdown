最近写博客的频率下降了好多，但其实很多变化都是以很缓慢的节奏在发生：从突发奇想出一个点子，在脑袋里酝酿，在社交网络上刷到新的灵感，借鉴又推翻，在拖延中迈出第一步第二步第三步，犯下一些愚蠢的小错误但也无所谓了，最终如愿以偿，开心。后面可能又有点怀疑自己当时的决定，但是也没太大所谓，然后下一个目标就出现了，循环往复。

上周末刚完成的一个小心愿就是烫发！从去年九月在国内最后一次理发之后已经过去半年了，自然状态下脸差不多已经要被头发遮完了，必须要全天戴着发箍。一般来说还好，我也觉得挺好看的，但是总有碍事的时候，烦躁起来就会有点泄气。虽然买了直板夹，但是每天早上打理费事不说，我生疏的技术还导致每次出来的结果基本等于开盲盒。因此萌生了想要烫发的想法。

但是烫发这种我从来没尝试过的事，是不可能一蹴而就的。首先，我先约了检查视力，配隐形眼镜，这样高度近视的我在发廊不至于变成睁眼瞎。然后，因为华人理发店烫发的价格都太高了，所以我问了经常做发型的越南同事，请她推荐了发廊，打电话过去果然便宜好多，立马愉快预约了。

说起来就是几句话的事，但其实光这两步应该就花了我一个月的时间。不过万事俱备，等了好久终于等到这天！上周六我起了个大早开车去离我家半小时车程的发廊，结果走到地方才惊觉我还戴着框架眼镜，忘记换成隐形了！当下真是心如死灰，但是预约时间已到，所以我就硬着头皮进去了。

坐定之后给理发师看了在instagram上找的reference，她一脸胸有成竹的样子。先是稍微剪了一下，然后上卷发夹。事实证明其实戴了隐形也没用，因为根本没办法从卷发夹的分布预判出最终的效果。接着上药水，过一会之后在吹暖风的罩子下面坐着烘，最后拆发夹洗头。她戴发夹的时候就拽头发拽得很紧，拆发夹的时候更疼，我只能无声地呲牙咧嘴，感觉像受酷刑一样。

吹干头发，忐忑地戴上眼镜，我莫名有点不敢看，匆匆瞟了两眼就逃出来了。回到家再照镜子，一开始有点失望，因为跟我给她看的reference完全两模两样。不过这两天又越看越顺眼了，不一样又怎样～而且现在不需要戴发箍了，打理也简单了，实用性也给到一个夯。理发师说能维持三个月，我还挺期待这中间随着头发长长又会有什么变化的。

最后，照理应该展示一下最终效果，但是出于隐私上的考虑就算了。不过我很不要脸地觉得有点像nmixx里的bae，就请勉为其难地带入我的脸想象一下吧！

Recently I’ve been blogging a lot less, but that doesn’t mean nothing is happening. If anything, a lot of changes are just moving at a very slow pace. An idea pops up, sits in my head for a while, then gets shaped as I scroll through social media. I pick up some inspiration, throw some of it away, and eventually start taking small steps. I make a few dumb mistakes along the way, but it’s fine. In the end, things usually work out, and I’m happy.

Later I might look back and question that decision a little, but not enough to matter. Then a new idea shows up, and the whole cycle starts again.

One small wish I finally checked off last weekend: getting a perm.

It had been six months since my last haircut in China last September. My hair had grown so long that it was basically covering my whole face, and I had to wear a headband all day. Most of the time I thought it looked pretty good, but there were definitely moments when it got in the way and started to annoy me.

I did buy a flat iron, but styling my hair every morning was a hassle. And with my very questionable skills, the result felt like opening a mystery box every time. That’s when the idea of getting a perm started to form.

But something I had never tried before wasn’t going to happen overnight.

First, I booked an eye exam and got contact lenses, so I wouldn’t be completely blind at the salon. Then, since Chinese salons here are quite expensive for perms, I asked a Vietnamese coworker who always has great hair for recommendations. She gave me a place, I called them, and the price was much better, so I booked it immediately.

It sounds like just a couple of steps, but honestly, these alone probably took me about a month.

Finally, last Saturday, everything was ready. I woke up early and drove about half an hour to the salon. And only when I got there did I realize I was still wearing my glasses and had forgotten to switch to contacts.

At that moment I was a bit like… okay, great. But the appointment had already started, so I just went in anyway.

I showed the stylist a reference I found on Instagram, and she looked very confident. She trimmed my hair a bit, then started putting in the rollers. In reality, even if I had worn contacts, it wouldn’t have helped much, because there’s no way to guess the final result from how the rollers are placed.

Then came the chemicals, and I sat under a warm air machine for a while. When she took the rollers out, it hurt even more than when she put them in. I just sat there silently baring my teeth, feeling like I was going through some kind of low-level torture.

After blow-drying my hair, I put my glasses back on. I was weirdly a bit scared to look, so I only glanced quickly and left the salon.

When I got home and finally looked in the mirror, I felt a little disappointed at first. It looked quite different from the reference I showed her.

But over the next couple of days, it started to grow on me. Different is fine. I don’t need a headband anymore, and it’s much easier to manage, which already feels like a big win.

The stylist said it would last about three months. I’m actually curious to see how it changes as my hair grows out.

Normally I would show the final result here, but for privacy reasons I’ll skip that.

That said, I will shamelessly say it kind of looks like Bae from NMIXX. So just imagine my face there and we’re good.
