可能是因为最近的生活实在是太顺了，所以竟然会产生一种不安全感，不知道这种幸福的生活什么时候就会突然终结。当真的有一点风吹草动发生时，就会很慌张。

从宏观上看，今年跟Leo的感情特别好。不是那种大起大落的激情，而是一种在稳定中慢慢升温的亲密。一起去了墨西哥和欧洲，马上又要去巴西。公寓慢慢变得更好看，墙上挂起了越来越多我们在旅行时拍的照片。

从微观上看，就比如上周末，过得就非常充实。周六早晨Leo去健身，我去商场修眼镜，顺便逛街买衣服。最近我花钱的心态也变了，一方面是大的层面上不奢望买房，所以没什么压力；另一方面最近股市表现很好，而且工资还涨了。
同时从欧洲回来的确更喜欢打扮了，加上爱淘便宜货的天性，总之各种因素叠加下最近很喜欢逛街和研究穿搭，Leo也持续在给我正反馈（比如我烫发之后）。总之周六在old navy买了好几件便宜货，还在优衣库花大价钱买了一条短裤，都是为去巴西做准备的。

中午Leo去和他的学生吃饭，我从商场回来简单煮了个泡面（与研究穿搭不同，做饭就没有这些有利因素支撑我继续深入下去，包括最近连运动都停了）。然后出发一起去三番看《霸王别姬》，甚至在路上的聊天都很开心，感觉现在跟Leo在一起就是完全放松的状态。

电影其实没有我印象当中那么宏大了，而且观众素质不太好。出来之后我挺累了，又饿，还有点不开心，然后去了任天堂店，什么也没买。晚饭去了戴利Daly City吃了个日式炸鸡，我其实感觉一般，但还是吃撑了，回家处于半死状态。但是最后还做爱了，第一次一起用了飞机杯。

周日早晨Leo去跟朋友爬山、吃饭，我去了宜家。装饰房间和研究穿搭算是我最近的两大兴趣点，去之前就列好了一个单子，再加上最近在社交网络上看了那么多家居博主的视频，所以在宜家如鱼得水，大件是买了新地毯，此外小件也买了好多，最后花了六百多。（Leo完全信任我，而我是在这些点上很想掌控一切的人，这也让我觉得我们很配）。

回来之后好累，吃了昨晚打包的剩饭就去睡午觉了。等我睡醒Leo也回来了，一起喝了咖啡，把新地毯铺上，把老地毯铺到卧室。然后去了jt和Jorge家，跟他们一起吃了晚饭，聊得也很开心。回家之后一起订了在里约的酒店，周末结束。

其实周日晚上的时候就想，这种轻松美好的生活，我真的可以一直拥有吗。然后晚上开始拉肚子，周一就觉得很虚弱，同时大的层面上会担心自己的健康。心情不好开始摸鱼，一直在玩手机拖时间，本来想早回家（工资和股票都在涨，同时工作又很轻松，这隐隐也让我惶恐），但是同事突然说要写两篇paper晚上就要提交，包括自己项目那边也有一堆事。回到家之后休息了一下，玩游戏连输好多把，接着开始写paper，开会。然后又传来妈妈检查结果不好可能需要化疗的消息，而且她又是在瞒着我，是四姨告诉我的。Leo因为考试周所以要到八点半才回来。家里很冷。仿佛一旦不顺的话，所有事情都像大山一样压过来了。

第二天写完之后再看，一方面我的情绪是真实的，另一方面也有很多假想敌是虚拟出来的。目前唯一不可控制的就是妈妈的病情，除此之外，跟Leo的感情很稳定，工作上的困难也都可以应付，财务上也不紧张，也不用急着担心和爸妈出柜或者怎么给爸爸养老。但是我的确一直在躲避去查癌症相关的资料，只要一看心里就堵得慌。


Maybe it’s because life has been going too smoothly lately, but I’ve started to feel this strange sense of insecurity. Like I don’t know when this comfortable, happy life might suddenly fall apart. And whenever even a small disturbance shows up, I get disproportionately anxious.

On a macro level, things with Leo have been really good this year. Not the kind of intense ups and downs, but something quieter — a steady kind of closeness that keeps deepening over time. We went to Mexico and Europe together, and we’re about to go to Brazil. The apartment is slowly becoming nicer, with more and more photos from our trips going up on the walls.

On a micro level, take last weekend as an example — it felt very full.
On Saturday morning, Leo went to the gym while I went to the mall to fix my glasses and ended up shopping for clothes. My attitude toward spending has also changed a bit recently. On a bigger level, I’ve kind of given up on the idea of buying a house anytime soon, so there’s less pressure. On a smaller level, the stock market has been doing well, and my salary has gone up.
Also, ever since coming back from Europe, I’ve been more interested in how I dress. Combined with my natural tendency to hunt for cheap finds, I’ve been enjoying shopping and figuring out outfits lately. Leo has been giving me consistent positive feedback too, especially after I got my perm. So on Saturday, I bought a bunch of cheap stuff at Old Navy, and then somehow spent a lot more at Uniqlo on a pair of shorts, all in preparation for Brazil.

At noon, Leo went to lunch with his students, and I went home and made instant noodles. Unlike my interest in fashion, cooking doesn’t really have any external motivation to push me forward. Besides, I’ve even stopped working out recently.

After that, we met up and went to San Francisco to watch Farewell My Concubine. Even the conversation on the way there was fun. Being with Leo now just feels very relaxed.

The movie didn’t feel as grand as I remembered, and the audience wasn’t great. Afterward I was tired, hungry, and a little annoyed. We went to the Nintendo store but didn’t buy anything. Dinner was fried chicken in Daly City — I didn’t think it was that good, but I still ate too much and went home feeling half-dead.
And somehow, the day still ended on a very intimate note.

Sunday morning, Leo went hiking and had lunch with friends, while I went to IKEA. Decorating the apartment and figuring out outfits are probably my two main interests right now. I had already made a list before going, and after watching so many home decor videos recently, I felt very efficient there. I bought a new rug and a bunch of smaller items, and ended up spending over 600 dollars.

Leo completely trusts me with these things, and I also like having control in areas like this. It makes me feel like we’re a good match.

When I got home, I was exhausted. I ate leftovers and took a nap. When I woke up, Leo was back. We had coffee together, set up the new rug, and moved the old one into the bedroom. Then we went to JT and Jorge’s place for dinner, had a good time chatting, and later booked hotels for Rio. Weekend over.

On Sunday night, I remember thinking: can this kind of easy, pleasant life really last?

That night I got diarrhea. On Monday I felt weak, and at a larger level I started worrying about my health. My mood dropped, and I began wasting time on my phone. I had planned to go home early — things have been going well financially, work has been relatively easy, how can this last forever? this makes me uneasy in a way.

But then a coworker suddenly said we needed to submit two papers that night. At the same time, my own project had a pile of tasks waiting. After getting home, I rested a bit, then played games and lost several matches in a row, which didn’t help. Then I started writing papers and joined meetings.

And then I heard that my mom’s latest test results weren’t good, and she might need chemotherapy. She hadn’t told me herself — I heard it from my aunt.

Leo didn’t get home until 8:30 because it’s exam week.
The apartment felt cold.

It was like once things started going wrong, everything piled up at once, pressing down all at the same time.
